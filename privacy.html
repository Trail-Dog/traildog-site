TrailDog — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Using TrailDog without an account

You can record rides without signing in. Everything stays in a private database on your device, and deleting the app deletes it. The only things that leave your phone are the network requests described under Services we send data to.

What we collect while you ride

While you are recording a ride — and only then — TrailDog collects your location (GPS route, speed, altitude) and motion-sensor data (accelerometer, gyroscope, barometer). These are what produce your ride stats, your descent scores and your Rider Index.

Background location

Recording continues while your phone is locked or in your pocket — that is what the "always"-style location permission is for. Location is collected only while a ride you started is recording. TrailDog does not track you at any other time.

What we store on our server, if you have an account

Creating an account turns on sync. From then on the following is stored on our servers:

AccountYour email address — or the private relay address Apple gives us if you use Sign in with Apple — and which sign-in method you used.
ProfileYour display name, your handle, your home area if you set one, your Rider Index, level and seven attribute scores, and your profile photo.
RidesThe full ride: your GPS route at recording precision, motion-sensor data, timings, distance and elevation, computed scores, detected trail names, the place name and the weather summary.
DescentsEach scored descending run — score, length, drop, duration, and an identifier derived from its geometry. This is what personal bests and comparison with friends are built on.
FriendsWho you have sent requests to, who has sent them to you, and who you are friends with.
DevicesAn install identifier, your device name, model, iOS version and app version, so we can tell which build a problem came from.
LogsTechnical records of what the app did — see Diagnostic logs.

What other people can see

  • Your profile photo, display name and handle can be seen by any signed-in TrailDog rider. That is how riders find each other by handle, and how a friend request shows who it is from.
  • Your Rider Index, attribute scores and ride summaries are visible to riders you have accepted as friends.
  • Your GPS routes are never visible to anyone else. Ride tracks are readable only by the account that recorded them.
  • None of it is public on the open internet — all of the above requires being signed in to TrailDog.

Diagnostic logs

The app records technical logs — sync results, errors, timings, app version and ride identifiers — and uploads them with your account so we can diagnose crashes and sync failures. Before anything is written, email addresses, authentication tokens and long key-like strings are stripped out automatically, and precise coordinates are never written to logs at all. Logs are kept for 90 days and then deleted.

Services we send data to

  • Supabase — hosts our database, file storage and sign-in. Everything in the table above is stored there on our behalf.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, if you choose it; geocoding, to turn your ride's start point into a place name; and Apple Maps imagery.
  • Open-Meteo — receives approximate ride coordinates and returns the weather for your ride. No account or identifier is sent.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API) — receives your ride's bounding box to match the trails you rode. No account or identifier is sent. Trail data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell or rent your data.

Sharing and backups — always your choice

The share button exports a ride file containing its GPS route, sensor data and scores, so you can send it to whoever you choose. Be aware that a ride file contains your precise route, which may start near your home. The Export Backup button creates a file you control. Your device's own iOS/iCloud backup may also include the app's data, governed by Apple's terms. Nothing is ever shared automatically.

Deleting your data

Delete Account in Settings removes your account and everything listed above: profile, rides, descents, friendships, device records, logs, and every stored file — ride tracks, raw sensor archives and your profile photo. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke that connection with Apple. This cannot be undone.

Rides already on your phone stay there until you delete the app, which removes the local copy.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU: we process your ride and profile data to provide the app you asked for (performance of a contract), and diagnostic logs to keep it working (legitimate interests). You have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data. Export Backup and Delete Account do the last two immediately from inside the app; for anything else, contact us below.

Children

TrailDog is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
