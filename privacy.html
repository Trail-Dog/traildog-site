Trail Dog — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026

The short version: your rides stay on your phone. Trail Dog has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no server. Nothing leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.

What the app collects, and where it lives

While recording a ride, Trail Dog collects your location (GPS route, speed, altitude) and motion-sensor data (accelerometer, gyroscope, barometer) to compute your ride stats and progression. All of it is stored in a private database on your device only. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Background location

Recording continues while your phone is locked or in your pocket — that's what the "always"-style location permission is for. Location is only collected while you are actively recording a ride you started. The app never tracks you outside a recording.

Network requests the app makes

Trail Dog makes three kinds of anonymous, keyless requests to display ride information. None include your name, identifiers, or full ride data:

  • Weather — your approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to fetch current conditions.
  • Place names — your ride's start coordinate is sent to Apple's geocoding service to name the ride's location.
  • Trail names — your ride's bounding box is sent to the OpenStreetMap Overpass API to match the trails you rode. Trail data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Map imagery is provided by Apple Maps as part of iOS.

Sharing — always your choice

The share button exports a ride file (its GPS route, sensor data and scores) so you can send it to whoever you choose — for example the developer, to help improve the scoring. Be aware a ride file contains your precise route, which may start near your home. Nothing is ever shared automatically.

Backups

The in-app Export Backup button creates a file you control. Your device's own iOS/iCloud backup may also include the app's data, governed by Apple's terms.

What Trail Dog doesn't do

  • No user accounts, no sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties

Children

Trail Dog is not directed at children under 13.
