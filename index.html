Trail Dog 🐕

A mountain-bike ride-progression app for iPhone. Record a ride with your phone in your pocket — get the payoff after.

Ride · Progress · Repeat

  • —Pocket recording

    Press START, pocket your phone, ride. Recording keeps going with the screen locked — no fiddling mid-trail.

  • —A celebration, not a spreadsheet

    Finish a ride and get a Rollup of what you actually achieved — the moments worth celebrating, front and centre.

  • —Honest stats

    Distance, climbing, top speed, steepest pitch, moving time — numbers that pass the sniff test against the ride you just did.

  • —The trails you actually rode

    Your ride is matched to real, named trails on the map — not just a line through the woods.

  • —Rider Index — eight attributes that grow

    A game-style rider profile with eight attributes — like Flow, Cornering and Air — that climb as you ride. Every gain traces back to something you did.

Coming to the App Store — September 2026 Free · iPhone · No ads

Your rides are yours. No ads, no selling your data, and nothing is shared unless you choose to share it. The privacy policy says exactly how it works, in plain English.
