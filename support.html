Trail Dog — Support

Trail Dog is a mountain-bike ride-progression app: record a ride, get a celebration of what you achieved, and watch your Rider Index grow. Ride · Progress · Repeat.

Get help

Email jackmecklenburgh@gmail.com — include what you were doing and, if it's about a specific ride, use the share button on that ride's detail screen to attach the ride file.

Common questions

Recording stopped when my screen locked? Trail Dog needs location access set to "Always" (Settings → Trail Dog → Location) for reliable pocket recording.

Where is my data? On your phone, nowhere else. Use Export Backup (Profile screen) to make a copy you control — deleting the app deletes your rides.

A stat looks wrong? Useful to hear — this app's scoring improves from real ride feedback. Share the ride file with a note about what felt off.
